Setup Guide

Get up and running in 5 minutes

Step 1 — Configure SMTP (Hostinger)▼
🟠 Hostinger Email setup
  1. Log in to hPanel (hpanel.hostinger.com) → Emails → Manage
  2. Create an email account if you haven't already (e.g. akshat.raj@justwords.in)
  3. Go to SMTP Config in the sidebar and click the Hostinger preset
  4. Enter your full email address as the username
  5. Enter the email account password (set in hPanel — NOT your hPanel login password)
  6. Use port 465 with SSL (pre-filled by the preset)
  7. Optionally set global CC/BCC addresses (e.g. a CRM inbox)
  8. Click Test Connection to verify
⚠️ This tool sends emails directly from your browser via a local backend server. You need server.js running. See "Running the Backend" below.
Step 2 — Prepare your CSV▼

Your CSV must have at least these two columns:

email,first_name,company john.doe@acme.com,John,Acme Corp sarah@xyz.com,Sarah,XYZ Ltd ceo@startup.io,Rahul,StartupIO

Save as .csv (UTF-8). Column order doesn't matter. Extra columns are ignored.

Step 3 — Customize templates & attachments▼

Go to Email Templates. All templates are pre-filled from your Justwords document. Edit subject lines and body text as needed. Use {{First Name}} for personalization. Each template has its own attachment section — upload a corporate deck, brochure, or any file to attach only to that specific email. Attachments are stored in-memory and must be re-uploaded each session.

Step 4 — Set timing & launch▼

Adjust follow-up delays in Sequences & Timing. Then go to Launch Campaign. You can set per-send CC/BCC overrides here. Always send a test email first (Test Mode is on by default). Once satisfied, turn off test mode and launch.

Step 5 — Manage responses & follow-ups▼

Use the Contact Tracker to update statuses:

  • Booked Call → sends Call Confirmation email immediately
  • Call Done → sends Post-Call Next Steps email
  • Not Interested → sends Graceful Exit email
  • No Response → follow-ups queue automatically based on timing

Click Send Due Follow-ups each day (or schedule it) to send pending follow-ups.
